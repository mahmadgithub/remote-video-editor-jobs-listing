Tips

  • Keep two reels ready: AI UGC ads and real estate reels. Name the tools you used (HeyGen, Higgsfield, CapCut, Premiere).
  • Set job alerts on LinkedIn, Indeed and Upwork with the keywords above. Reply within hours.
  • Many employers use a paid test task. That is normal. Don't do long unpaid work.
  • Several "worldwide" roles need US or UK hours. Check the overlap.
  • Never pay to apply. Never share ID or bank details before a signed agreement.
  • Use your real country on marketplaces. Don't use a VPN or fake address to get around location rules.
